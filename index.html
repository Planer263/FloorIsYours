01 / One piece each

Each player gets an 18-second turn to add a piece. Choose a tool, then click a legal position inside the dashed construction zone. Pieces persist for all five rounds. Start, checkpoint, and finish are protected.

02 / Race your mistakes

Run right and cross the finish. Jump over gaps. Air-dash to save a bad jump. Everyone shares the same obstacle geometry, but players don’t block each other. Falling or hitting a trap respawns you after a short delay.

03 / Score the chaos

Finishers earn 10, 7, 5, then 3 points. Your spikes/hammer earn 2 points per different rival hit, once per race. No points for hitting yourself. Highest total after five rounds wins; ties are shared.

Know your pieces

Platform: a solid landing.
Spikes: dangerous at every angle.
Swing hammer: a moving hazard with a solid support ledge.
Updraft: holds you aloft while you steer.
Blink ledge: solid for 2.5 seconds, gone for 1.5. Pulses before disappearing.

Controls

A/D or arrows move. Space or ↑ jumps. Shift dashes. R respawns. F toggles full-course/follow view. During your build turn use 1–5 to choose, arrows to position the preview and Enter to place. Escape closes dialogs. Touch has separate building and racing buttons.

Know your limits

The checkpoint is at the middle island. Touch it to respawn there. Construction can make a course obnoxious or impossible—there is no solvability guarantee. Skip your turn if you’re feeling merciful. No in-game chat, voting, or replay system.

Online simulation continues while menus are open. Keep the host tab visible. Public signaling is used; restrictive networks may need a TURN relay. Losing the host ends the match. Disconnected guests are removed from remaining turns/races.
